📘 Study Tips to Bag Distinctions 🎓

  • 📅 Plan Ahead: Create a term-based revision calendar and break down topics weekly.
  • 📖 Understand Concepts: Don’t just memorize — explain concepts to someone else or teach it.
  • 🧪 Practice Diagrams: Label biological diagrams often — they’re easy marks!
  • 📈 Use Past Papers: Attempt at least 1 past paper per week. Review your answers critically.
  • 🔍 Focus on Keywords: Highlight action verbs in questions like "describe", "explain", or "state".
  • 🎥 Watch Revision Videos: Use YouTube or platforms like Mindset Learn for visual understanding.
  • 📝 Summarize Notes: Create one-pager summaries or mind maps for quick revision before exams.
  • 💬 Join Study Groups: Collaborate and quiz each other — group accountability boosts performance.
  • ⏳ Pomodoro Method: Study in 25-min bursts with 5-min breaks. Great for focus and retention.
  • 😴 Rest & Hydrate: Don’t overwork. Get enough sleep, eat well, and take breaks to recharge. 🧘‍♀️
